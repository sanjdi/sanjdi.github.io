Banking App

A simple term deposit final balance calculator built on Node.js with TypeScript. It provides 2 endpoints to calculate final balance.

The Term Deposit Final Balance Calculator is a straightforward tool designed to compute the final balance of an investment based on the initial deposit amount, interest rate, investment term, compounding frequency, and interest payment method. This calculator helps investors quickly estimate the growth of their term deposits.

The calculator uses the provided parameters to compute the final balance using financial formulas for compound interest. The result depends on whether the interest is reinvested (compounding) or taken as income. It offers a clear and detailed calculation, empowering users to make informed investment decisions.
